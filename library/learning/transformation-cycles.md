# Transformation Cycles

Change often involves repeated phases of experimentation, disruption, learning, and integration.

## Core Principles
- Development is rarely linear.
- Setbacks can contain useful information.
- Letting go of outdated strategies is part of adaptation.

## Practices
- Ask: "What am I shedding?" and "What am I stepping into?"
- Review completed cycles, not just new beginnings.
- Use setbacks as data for recalibration.
