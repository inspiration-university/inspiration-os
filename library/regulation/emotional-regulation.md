# Emotional Regulation

Inspired living requires emotional agility — not suppression, not indulgence.

## Core Principles
- Emotions are messengers, not enemies.
- Resisting emotions can amplify them.
- Naming a feeling can help create distance from it.

## Practices
- Journal by naming the emotion, trigger, and desired response.
- Use slow breathing as a simple down-regulation practice.
- Check in with your emotional state during the day.
