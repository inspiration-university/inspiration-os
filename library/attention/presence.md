# Presence

Presence is the ability to deliberately return attention to current experience.

## Core Principles
- Attention is limited and trainable.
- Rumination about the past and anticipation of the future can pull attention away from current experience.
- Presence does not require ignoring the past or future; it means choosing where attention goes now.

## Practices
- Daily body scans or sensory check-ins.
- Deliberate periods without notifications.
- Use grounding exercises when attention becomes overwhelmed.
