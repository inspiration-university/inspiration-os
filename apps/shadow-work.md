# Shadow Work

Shadow work is a reflective practice for examining traits, emotions, impulses, or memories that a person tends to avoid, reject, or disown.

## Prompts
- What do I judge most strongly in others?
- When did I last feel ashamed, and what meaning did I assign to it?
- What might I be avoiding because it is uncomfortable to acknowledge?

## Practice
- Set aside uninterrupted reflection time.
- Record observations before explanations.
- Approach the material with curiosity rather than self-punishment.

This is a reflective tool, not a substitute for professional care when distress is severe.
