# Morning Rituals

A morning ritual is a repeatable startup sequence for attention, energy, and intention.

## Components
- Silence: meditation, reflection, prayer, or nature.
- Movement: stretching, yoga, walking, or other activity.
- Expression: journaling, music, or speaking aloud.
- Alignment: review values, vision, and immediate priorities.

The ritual should be adapted to the person rather than treated as a universal prescription.
