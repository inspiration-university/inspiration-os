# Vision Mapping

Vision mapping makes a desired future concrete enough to inspect, revise, and act on.

## Instructions
- Map major domains: relationships, work, health, learning, creativity, community.
- Write a short letter from a plausible future self.
- Revisit the map regularly and revise assumptions as reality changes.

## Prompts
- What excites me even if I do not yet know how to achieve it?
- What kind of world or community do I want to help create?
- Which parts of this vision depend on me, and which do not?
